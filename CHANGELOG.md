# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First version.

- Boards, each starting with To do / Doing / Done; columns can be added, renamed, moved and deleted.
- Cards with a title, notes and an optional due date, dragged between and within columns (mouse, or
  a short press on touch), or moved from the card editor.
- Push notifications when a card comes due, per device, from Settings > Notifications; a test push
  to check a device is set up.
- Installable PWA with an offline app shell; light, dark and automatic themes; phone layout with a
  bottom tab bar and swipeable columns.
- Accounts with email and password; registration behind `REGISTRATION_ENABLED`.
